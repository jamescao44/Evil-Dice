Smaller effects, smoother third level, seven build checks

Implemented the updated relics, Steel Sword at +3 multiplier, and Prismatic Stone at +30 total base. Percentage base bonuses apply in inventory order. Pet Orb fuses all five pets into one slot.

Visuals and progression

Damage scaling now uses 1 + 0.25 × log10(damage / reference), with minimum 1×. References reset to 20 / 100 / 300 at the three level boundaries. Attack effects cap at 1.4×; floating numbers cap at 1.6×. Equipment changes never reset the reference.

Waves 21–30 now alternate pressure and relief, with 50% more enemy souls. The final boss is LV.11 with a shorter first phase. Deluxe Meal now adds 8 base and +0.5 multiplier; Super Feast adds 15 base and ×1.3, retaining ingredient effects. These changes reduce both under-levelled stalls and the five-piece meal jump.

Across 360 fixed-inventory encounters, the modest-build final-boss median fell from 49.5 to 14 enemy turns; meal-only fell 17→9 and meal-synergy 8.5→6. All 360 revised encounters cleared. This tests combat pacing, not human win probability.

What needs support

Single target and status have distinct advantages. Raw damage remains broadly strong: replacing Steel Sword with Alternating Gears reduces its single-target result from 106k to 56k. Putting Steel Sword into the item build instead of Pet Ghost raises 24k to 63k. Even at +3 it remains unusually attractive across builds. Burning itself contributes only 2.8k of the status build’s 126k group damage; explosions do most of the work.

Item attacks, occasional burst and one-roll play need more identity. The burst build’s average largest turn is only 9.9k, versus raw’s 27.3k. More unconditional multipliers would make those builds look alike.

  • Proc Dynamo, epic: item attack damage +75%, manual skill damage −25%. More item power with a clear cost; no extra trigger chains.
  • Reserve Magazine, rare: at turn end, spend unused rerolls to repeat the first roll’s auto volley once per reroll. Spent rolls give no treasure progress; repeats trigger no item attacks. Gives one-roll play a damage-versus-loot decision.
  • Overload Battery, epic: skills deal −25% damage for two turns; the third turn’s first manual attack deals ×3. A visible charge offers a short, strong burst window.
  • Scar Charm, rare: self-inflicted HP loss charges the next manual skill by the HP lost, capped at 10% max HP per turn. Spent once, without reapplying multipliers. Rewards managing injury rather than unlimited healing loops.
  • Smouldering Incense, epic: Burning gains damage based on character base, but explosions cannot trigger. A separate damage-over-time route instead of further amplifying Powder Keg.

These five proposals are not implemented. Values are starting points for targeted tests, not established balance.
