Evil Dice · 25 October 2026

Two levels. More room for enemies to act.

The campaign now has two ten-wave levels. Enemy durability grows much faster in level two, while incoming damage stays restrained. Bosses can finish charges more often without making every unavoidable hit lethal.

Implemented

Start continues your build

Finish the first boss and collect all rewards, then press Start Level 2. Returning to the home screen and pressing Start does the same. Levels, XP, Blood Gems, relic order/counters and permanent tarots carry over.

Level two begins at full health with temporary combat effects, Combo and skill charge reset. Defeat or leaving the run restarts from that level’s saved entry build; gains from a failed attempt do not carry back. The checkpoint survives closing the game. Completing both levels makes the next Start a fresh run.

What changed in combat

First level: more health through the middle and final waves. Commander phase HP is now 1,950 / 2,860, up from 810 / 1,214. Its charged hit is 69 damage.

Second level: a substantially higher health curve, with easier encounters between pressure waves. Spellsword phase HP is 10,500 / 12,600, up from 2,489 / 3,042. Its charged hit is 60 damage.

Boss damage was kept slightly below its previous values because enemies now get more actions. Spellsword’s first-phase heal fell from 20% to 10% of maximum HP to prevent excessive stalling. Player growth, XP, relic effects and loot rates are unchanged in this pass.

Enemy health multiplier · grey: previous · orange: current

Multipliers apply to each enemy’s base stats. Boss base HP also increased. Difficulty comes from the authored wave composition as well as these curves; it does not adapt secretly to the player’s build.

How the tuning converged

  1. Raise durability first. The initial boss-health increase created fights that were too long for weaker builds.
  2. Trim boss health and add the level checkpoint. This kept a meaningful second level without sending a loss back to wave one.
  3. Reduce sustained frustration. Cut Spellsword healing to 10% and softened the final elite wave’s damage.
  4. Check stronger real inventories. Preserve fused meals in the test policy and increase late level-two durability again. A damage increase was not needed.

What still needs watching

Strong combinations can still beat a boss without taking damage, especially with frequent Perfects. The fixed Dragon + copy-relic stress build clears the first boss in a median of 6 throws and the second in 11. That is a multi-item payoff, but future balance should inspect stacking synergies before applying another blanket health increase.

Some weaker builds still have long boss fights. Headless tests cannot judge animation pace, phone timing or how repetitive those fights feel. The next useful check is a normal phone run focused on boss duration and the relief between pressure waves.
