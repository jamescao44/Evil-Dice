Enemy levels & a rebuilt campaign

Every enemy now has its own level. Both ten-wave levels use authored roles, counts and mixed levels, so a tougher lead enemy can be paired with weaker support. New identities arrive individually; familiar encounters give players time to absorb them.

What changed and why

  • No shared wave multiplier. A unit’s role, elite flag and level fully determine its spawn HP, attack and drops. Values round once. Boss phases reuse that level; Commander guards use their own lower level.
  • One unfamiliar enemy at a time. All five roles appear normally before their elite versions. First appearances and bosses are guaranteed combat waves. Shops retain the first offer after wave 3 and at least two intervening battles.
  • Stronger leads, weaker support. Testing exposed an early overcrowded wave and later elites that disappeared before showing their behavior. The revision reduced wave 3 and raised individual introduction leaders instead of adding more enemies.
  • Updated special-unit rules. Normal Paladins have attack, shield and rest only. Elite Paladins charge twice and strike twice on the following turn. Elite High Priests charge twice and strike twice immediately.
  • Readable levels. Level and HP share the health bar. Holding an enemy shows its level and base soul/gem drops. The home map and CLI use the same authored roster.

Level 5 Vanguard: 214 HP · 8 damage · 7 souls · 7 gems. Level 10 Vanguard: 795 HP · 10 damage · 10 souls · 10 gems. Player leveling, relic balance and chest reward rates are unchanged by this pass.
