EVIL DICE · 25 October 2026 · CLI comparison

Relics grow more slowly; strong builds still snowball

The document changes made a measurable difference: median full-run throws rose 12.5%, from 108 to 121.5, and enemy turns rose 23.5%, from 34 to 42. All 12 runs still cleared both levels in each version. Inventory filled at almost the same point.

The wave-20 representative build now needs 56.5 throws instead of 36.5. That is meaningful resistance, but also a warning that a modestly equipped late boss may feel lengthy. It is not a reason to increase enemy HP further without a GUI pacing check.

What remains

Relic acquisition barely changed: the first full inventory moved from wave 11 to 11.5. If filling the backpack too quickly is still the concern, reward frequency remains a separate tuning lever. Strong multiplication and copying combinations still dominate some runs: seed 601 finished in 52 throws after the changes, versus 47 before, with Mithril Blade, Ancient Tentacle, Magic Sword and a fused meal in its new build.

At level one, a 1-2-3 Fireball with the four-piece meal falls from 378 to 216 damage; the full five-piece meal falls from 891 to 507. These examples exclude Perfect, other relics and target modifiers. Light Sword is a deliberate buff in the document: against a sole enemy it adds +2, rather than multiplying by 1.5, so it can counteract some nerfs during boss fights.

Verification and limits

2,112 isolated encounter attempts and 24 full runs completed with no CLI command errors. In each version, all 12 passive-only Dragon tests against wave 20 reached the 600-command cap; they are censored, not counted as clears. Focused tests cover relic stats, meal fusion, left-to-right modifiers, clover eligibility and probability, tarot precedence, displayed damage, localization, rewards, shops and both layouts. Existing shutdown resource warnings remain in the CLI-session and encounter suites; their assertions pass.

The CLI does not measure finger input, animation pacing, audio, or human win probability. Enemy stats, XP progression and reward frequency were not changed in this pass.
