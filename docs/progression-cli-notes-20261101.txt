Progression CLI notes — 2026-11-01

Six seeded campaigns: 112011, 112029, 112047, 112083, 112109, 112157.
Four cleared; one died at boss 2, one at boss 3. No engine/action errors.
Gameplay source stayed unchanged throughout the test.

Conditions: 35% Perfect initial throws; collect all chest tiers and prizes;
use tarots and gemstones; prioritize food sets and ranked shop relics.
The heuristic does not buy shop tarots/soul bottles or refresh shops.
This is a small sample, not a paired before/after comparison or a test of
animation feel. Skill counts include each individual cast, including bonuses.

Observations
- Paladin debut at 1-7 is a clear step: 2–5 turns (median 3.5), with damage
  taken in 5/6 runs. The preceding wave usually took one turn.
- High Priest + Cannon at 2-5 took 2–4 turns; Cannon + Bomber at 3-3 took
  2–6. However, 4/6 and 4/5 runs respectively took no damage there. Cannons
  fired only once across all six campaigns: interruption/killing still wins
  reliably for this policy. Longer fights do not always mean more pressure.
- Commander phase 1 took a median 4.5 turns. Phase 2 took 6–13 (median 8.5):
  the full-strength Paladin reinforcements make this phase drag beyond the
  roughly four-turn target. This is the first pacing adjustment I would make.
- Boss 2's cleared phases had medians of 4.5 and 7 turns. Its fatal heavy hit
  dealt 726 to a player with 600/1100 HP and Corrosion: not a full-health kill.
- Boss 3's cleared phases had medians of 4.5 turns each, but the weakest run
  died after 23 turns in phase 1. Late ordinary waves mostly took 1–2 turns.
  Build variance remains large: all four clears finished with Super Meal;
  the final-boss failure had Budget Meal. This does not prove the set is required.
- Optional soul rewards shrink the intended level gaps. All six players
  entered 1-7 at LV5 against LV5 enemies, above the kill-only LV3 reference.

Suggested next pass: shorten Commander phase 2 first, then address weak-build
boss attrition and low-risk late ordinary waves. Avoid a blanket enemy buff.
No balance changes were made during this test.
