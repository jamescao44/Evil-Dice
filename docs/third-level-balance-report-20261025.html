What changed and why

  • Enemy levels rise in steps. Level-two main threats reach LV.9, 11 and 13. Level three uses LV.14, 16 and 18 pressure groups. These encounters cannot be replaced by a shop; support units still mix levels.
  • Health rises faster than damage. The existing per-enemy scaling stays ×1.30 HP and ×1.04 damage per level, with linear soul/gem growth. Stronger enemies survive more hits without proportionally multiplying unavoidable damage.
  • Remove healing loops, keep sturdy enemies. Two stress-test lineups reached 37–54 enemy phases when elite archers repeatedly healed a protected Paladin. Waves 21 and 27 now use normal archers. In the revised 35% Perfect cohort, both waves finish within 18 enemy phases.
  • Keep the boss increase measured. Spellsword moves from LV.10 to LV.11: 13,786 / 16,543 HP and 30 attack. Testing LV.12 made weaker builds drag. Fanatical Mage starts at LV.13, with 34,947 HP per phase.

The new boss

The Fanatical Mage summons damage-absorbing octopuses, splits them into tentacles and applies Darkness. Phase two replaces the small servants with a charging, taunting demon while the mage continues its move pool. Three separate skills interrupt the demon. Its pact consumes one fifth of the mage’s scaled health per enemy phase, so this phase also has a five-turn survival route.

Current defaults for the open clarification: at most six small servants; one servant absorbs each redirected hit without spill; summoned creatures grant no loot or kill-trigger bonuses; demon base attack 30, double on release; pact loss scales with the mage and continues while the demon is interrupted. These rules are isolated in the boss module for easy revision. Added four CC0 placeholder sprites and checked crowded formations in portrait and landscape.

What the tests show

The same 12 seeds and meal-priority policy changed level-two ordinary fights from a median of 1 to 4 enemy phases. Fights ending before any enemy phase fell from 22.4% to 4.8%. All 12 runs still cleared. Two additional 12-seed cohorts, at 35% and 15% assumed Perfect success, also cleared all 30 waves. Meal tiers varied naturally; no full set was injected into these runs.

Limits: this is one automated policy, not a human win-rate estimate. It skips Ultra relic prizes through normal forfeiture, retaining their currency/salvage; it does not prove every legendary build is balanced. Some weaker low-Perfect runs still need roughly 20–30 enemy phases for a boss or late pressure wave. GUI timing, tactile pacing and phone performance require human play. A successful powerful build can still clear individual waves immediately.
