Wave-4 progression test — 2026-11-01

Implemented
- Preserve existing wave-12 player/world proportions for all later battles.
- Commander guards: ordinary LV.1 Paladins, full 400 HP catalog stats.
- Target gaps repeat [0,0,-1,2,2,1,3,3], using kill-only expected XP and a
  previous-wave level floor across all three levels.
  Enemy levels: 1,1,1,4,5,5,7,7 / 7,7,7,10,10,10,13,13 /
  13,13,13,15,15,15,18,19.
- New specials at 1-4 (Paladin), 2-4 (High Priest/Cannon), 3-4 (Bomber).
- Future balance simulations default to 70% Perfect initial throws.

Six campaigns, all rewards collected: 5 cleared, 1 died at the final boss.
Same six seeds and decision policy as the prior test; both roster and Perfect
rate changed, so these results do not isolate either factor. No action/engine
errors; source unchanged during simulation. Later changes only adjust boss HUD
framing, not combat. GUI saves and persistent pity are untouched.

Brief observations
- First Paladin encounter: 4–7 turns (median 5), damage in 5/6 runs. It is a
  noticeable early obstacle immediately after the first shop.
- High Priest/Cannon at 2-4: 2–4 turns (median 4), damage in 3/6 runs.
- Bomber/Cannon at 3-4: 1–5 turns (median 1.5), no player damage in this sample.
- Commander phase medians: 3.5 / 6 turns. Phase 2 still exceeds the earlier
  four-turn aim, but this sample is shorter than the previous 8.5-turn median.
- Boss 2 phase medians: 6 / 5.5 turns. Boss 3 cleared-phase medians: 3 / 5.
  The failed run lasted 30 turns in boss 3's first phase: a long attrition tail.
- Later ordinary waves remain mostly 1–2 turns. The fourth-wave spike is
  clear early, weaker late; build differences still dominate late boss pacing.
