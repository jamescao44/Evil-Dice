# Evil Dice

A Godot 4 pixel-art dice game: grow an eldritch horror through **nineteen squad waves**, combining six dice into skills and earning Reroll Treasure from unused throws. City Fever is temporarily skipped.

The shared [Chinese Google Design Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i-PEouZmzwagpz6CTa7CjMVl_UJd99-io_uB7TMgguM/edit) is the primary record for game designs and changes. Write future design updates there in Mandarin (Simplified Chinese), keeping proposals separate from implemented features. The earlier English Google Doc and `docs/game-design.md` remain reference snapshots.

## Language

The interface defaults to 简体中文. Open **Settings** on the home screen, or the three-bar menu in battle, to choose **简体中文** or **English**. Changes apply immediately and are saved locally, including browser storage in web builds.

Basic dice use the supplied 48×48 pixel spritesheet. Each throw starts at a random rolling frame, plays forward or backward at a slightly different speed, and spins along a randomized curved path inside the dice area before returning to its hexagram position. Final faces and the counterclockwise landing order are preserved. Home and miniature skill previews use the same artwork; reduced effects keep rolls stationary.

Translations live in `assets/localization/ui.csv`. During local play in Godot, saving the CSV updates the running game in about a second—no manual reimport or restart. Keep the `keys` column and formatting placeholders unchanged. Invalid or incomplete saves keep the last valid translations and report the problem in Godot's output. Exported builds still bundle translations at build time; updating a published web game requires a new export/upload. See `docs/code-architecture.md` for implementation details.

## Play

Open `project.godot` in Godot and click **Run Project** for the portrait home screen. Open `scenes/home.tscn` and use **Run Current Scene** for the landscape home screen. The original `scenes/battle.tscn` and `scenes/battle_portrait.tscn` remain available for direct battle testing. Gameplay uses clicks or taps, with no keyboard controls.

The home screen has five bottom tabs, ordered **Dices / Ancients / Play / Summon / Shop**, with Play selected initially. The opening portrait, Ancient picker and character tab use the current level-one Flameling sprite. Ancients shows The Horror, its lore and play style, and a skill reference for automatic matching attacks, four fire straights and leftover healing. Swipe its portrait horizontally, drag with a mouse, or open the visual selection menu to browse the collection. Only The Horror is available; the carousel stays on it until more entries exist. Dices contains the basic Bone Dice, with a larger preview and descriptions on tap. The Play button displays the selected dice and starts a new nineteen-wave run in the same layout. Home in the battle grimoire or ending screen leaves the run and returns to Play, retaining the current session's selections and sound/effects/speed settings. Summon is a placeholder; Shop is blank. Blood Gems and Souls exist within the active run; summoning and saved collection progression are not implemented. Tarot is available from shops and relic effects.

1. Tap the center of the six-dice hexagram to charge, then again to throw all unused dice. There are **three throws per player turn**. Every face is independently uniform; Perfect uses **±35 ms** timing and builds the damage combo without guaranteeing matches. Perfect throws snap all unused dice into place simultaneously; ordinary throws retain quick counterclockwise reveals. Thrown dice show repeating outward pixel waves; only the longest available straights hint from high to low while nothing is selected.
2. Drag through or tap useful dice to select a straight (3–6 consecutive unique faces). Release a valid drag or tap **Confirm** to summon. Invalid additions shake; Cancel Selection clears the selection. While a summoned skill waits, all dice are disabled. Cast onto the battlefield or drag back to the red center to cancel and unlock its dice without refunding rolls. Saving skills is temporarily disabled.
3. **Using all six dice through skills grants another player turn:** six unused dice and three throws. Bonuses can chain; enemy moves, round-based statuses and relic counters wait. Unused throws bank Reroll Treasure once per completed pool. Click **End Turn** to heal for leftover pip sum × 1% of maximum HP, rounded once, then let enemies act; healing never earns a bonus turn. Frozen or untouched turns earn no treasure.
4. Every group of 2+ matching newly rolled dice automatically attacks random legal living enemies, one hit per matching die. Each hit deals **base × (0.5 + 0.1 × pip)** before modifiers; matching dice bounce and glow while firing, and remain available afterward. Only straights summon skills: **Fireball / Fiery Slash / Fire Storm / Brimstone** for 3/4/5/6 dice. Large matching groups have no additional rarity bonus yet.
5. Hold enemies to inspect their queued move and statuses. Fireball keeps its selected target for added uses; automatic matches choose a fresh living target per hit. After each battle, collect its chosen route reward and select the next destination. Wave nineteen ends the current run.

Top controls: ordinary animation speed, sound, and a **three-bar menu**. Debug includes **Spawn Normal Chest**, using the same 25% upgrade chain, shared pity counters, and real rewards without clearing the current wave or spending Reroll Treasure progress; closing it restores the current battle or held skill. The menu page contains **Debug**, **Effects: Full/Reduced**, **Help / Grimoire**, and **Resume**; Help and Debug return to this page when closed. Active statuses appear as small independent icons directly above the Horror, each with its own remaining attack casts, turns, burning stacks, next-throw stun count, or city-immunity seconds. Inactive or expired effects disappear individually. Both attack boons, invincibility, freeze, burning, stun, darkness, corrosion, and invisibility use separate indicators; both attack boons add their bonuses while active. Hover for explanations or tap in portrait. Help includes a dedicated status page with concise descriptions of all eight combat statuses. The menu page, Help and Debug pause the charge meter, city attacks, charging audio and skill effects. Losing window focus cancels an unfinished charge without spending a roll. Restart after victory or defeat begins a new run.

The former save slot now holds **End Turn**. Saved-skill implementation remains available only to legacy scripted flows.

## Combat and growth

Every skill is a `MultiHitSkill`. Fire straights start with one use; next-skill modifiers can add uses to non-instant attacks. Automatic matching groups use the matching dice count and retain combo-hit relic modifiers, without consuming next-skill tarot or a stored critical. All uses of a selected Fireball stay on its original target; automatic matches stop when no living enemy remains. Leftover healing is a single non-attack action and preserves offensive charges.
For future modifiers, call `model.set_skill_use_multiplier("echo_buff", 2)` for all skills, or `skill.set_use_multiplier("relic_echo", 2)` for one skill object. Passing `1` removes that named source; refreshing the same source replaces it, while different sources multiply together. Saved skills preserve their own modifiers and use current model modifiers when cast. The use budget freezes at cast start so an expiring modifier cannot change the remaining hits or orb count midway. New runs clear model modifiers. Twin Scythes and Cursed Bow supply ordered combo-use modifiers through this shared budget. For each wave cleared while owned, Cursed Bow adds one combo hit and compounds single-target damage by ×0.95 (`0.95^waves`); it has no penalty before the first clear and does not penalize area attacks.

Character base is **5 × 2^(level − 1)**, plus flat relic base bonuses. Multiply this by the automatic-match or straight coefficient, then by the ordinary damage multiplier. Boons add, relics apply in inventory order, and Perfect independently multiplies the result (first ×1.3, then +0.1 per combo). Target Burning bonuses apply before flooring damage. No old normal-attack cap applies.

| Straight | Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 dice | Fireball | One target: (3 + 0.1 × pip sum) × base; apply 2 Burning afterward. |
| 4 dice | Fiery Slash | All enemies: (2 + 0.1 × pip sum) × base; double against already-burning enemies. |
| 5 dice | Fire Storm | All enemies: (5 + 0.1 × pip sum) × base; double against already-burning enemies, then apply 2 Burning. |
| 6 dice | Brimstone | Kill all through shields and taunt; no extra healing. |

Fireball, flame arcs, a swirling fire storm and golden brimstone columns replace the character's tentacle effects. Intensity increases with straight length. Leftovers become healing embers; Darkness blocks their healing and recovery cannot exceed maximum HP.

Both attack boons add their +0.5 and +2.0 bonuses to DMG. They consume **one charge per completed attack cast**, covering all hits; saved attacks also consume one charge. Support skills, banking, skipped turns, tarot damage, relic procs and enemy phases do not consume them. Charges carry between waves and follow the same rule in City Fever. Reapplying the same attack boon emits a 10-base (33) or 50-base (333) shockwave using current modifiers, then refreshes its charges. Double-2 is now **Abyssal Frost**: one additive Frozen stack per living enemy, no damage or attack triggers. Strength increases the applied stacks; the city is immune. Triple-2 remains a full heal. Invincibility adds its new duration to the time remaining and counts down after the last enemy's action. In the city, immunity lasts **2 real-time seconds per stack**, including idle, charging and casting; menus/rewards pause it. Reapplying preserves a partially elapsed stack. Bonus casts do not advance turn clocks. Multi-hit actions finish their remaining hits against corpses after the last living foe dies. Consecutive attack hits gradually accelerate within each cast (+0.08× speed per hit, capped at 3×), shortening both travel and recovery while preserving all hits, damage and use counts. Each new cast starts at normal speed; the existing 2×/City Fever speed applies on top. The Chariot deals **50 fixed damage** to each enemy (or the city), unaffected by damage bonuses or damage multipliers, while respecting shields and retaining attack-relic effects.

Held attack skills show live per-hit damage as **base + modifier (xN)**; targeted skills show it when selected or dragged. For example, a level-one 1/2/3 Fireball without bonuses reads `18 + 0 (x1)`. The modifier includes the global multiplier, relics, attack boons, Perfect combo, critical charges, darkness, corrosion and normal target caps. Base is the integer part of the skill base; the final sum matches combat's rounded impact damage before squad shields and remaining HP. Hovering a target selects its cap; before choosing, differing caps show a modifier range. Cap reductions can produce a negative modifier. Saved previews use current stats. This is an extra red line above the bubble; the original attack description stays below it. Squad instant kills have no numeric line; city 111 and 666 remain numerical. Freeze has no numeric damage/healing line; full heal shows the actual missing health as green `+N HP`. Portrait skills settle slightly lower to leave room between this line and the treasure bank. Attack-buff captions use “for N attacks”; invincibility uses “for N turns” or city “for N seconds”. An active matching boon adds a short gold reapply-bonus line beneath the regular skill description.

After each battle, travel heals 25 HP without increasing maximum HP. Soul upgrades now own health growth. There is no automatic damage-multiplier increase between waves. Normal caps are ⅓ HP in stages 1–2, ½ in stage 3, and ¾ from stage 4 onward. Growth is fixed, with no mutation choices. The Horror grows steadily from 224×224 on wave 1 to 288×288 on wave 20. Enemy display size decreases steadily from 192 on wave 1 to 44 on wave 19 (the former wave 9 size); city troops remain 20. Paving shrinks from 96 to 16 and scenery from 128 to 16 over the nineteen squad waves; city scenery finishes at 12. During the 1.65-second growth transition, the feet stay planted as the Horror stretches up, compresses, swells slightly beyond its new size, then settles exactly. Reduced effects keeps the steady size increase without the bounce. Enemies form compact groups through the battlefield: landscape groups shift inward, while portrait groups use the upper center and open right flank, reserving room for the Horror and status icons. Health bars, intent icons and target areas remain separate.

The city has 20,000 HP and the same 1.0 damage baseline as squad waves. **666 deals 2000 × the full DMG multiplier**, limited by remaining city HP, using the same multiplier as other numeric attacks. At ×1.0, city 666 deals 2000 damage before flat relic bonuses. City 111 deals 5× regular attack power, not an instant city kill. AoE clears all troops; each rapid hit removes at most one troop. Heal and power-up triples do no damage and do not clear troops. Invincibility blocks both troop and ranged damage; city timers and reinforcements continue. Buildings and walls turn to rubble as health falls.

Enemies drop **5 Blood Gems normally, 8 for elites, 10 for Paladins/High Priests, and 15 for their elites**. City troops grant 5 each. Soul Vessel applies with fractional carry. Each death pays once, including burning, tarot executions and instant kills; corpse hits pay nothing. A few gems scatter outward, make two diminishing ground bounces, rest briefly with a faint white glow, then fly to the currency counter with sparkling chimes. The displayed balance counts up on arrival, and the last kill’s pickup finishes before wave rewards open.

## Perfect combo and Reroll Treasure

A **Perfect combo** counter sits opposite the saved skill slot, showing the actual independent Perfect damage multiplier (x1.0 at zero, x1.3 after the first Perfect), while keeping the streak internally. The total damage multiplier is shown separately in white at the battlefield’s top left. Each accepted Perfect adds one Combo. Its independent factor is **×1.0 at zero, otherwise ×(1.2 + 0.1 × Combo)**: ×1.3/×1.4/×1.5 for the first three. This multiplies the result of buffs and relics; combo 5+ burns with a warm glow and rising flames. The bonus applies to the skill from that throw and every subsequent hit, including saved skills at their current cast-time stats and city Annihilation. A non-Perfect throw resets it to zero. Canceling charge, using saved skills, claiming treasure, breaking ice, and changing waves preserve the streak; restarting clears it. The multiplier applies before integer rounding and normal damage caps. Healing and instant kills retain their existing behavior. Debug Perfects count once.

The **Reroll Treasure** bank unlocks chests at **3 / 8 / 15 total unused throws**. Turns that included a throw bank their unused rolls once, after enemies and statuses finish or when the wave clears early. Perfects and misses never change the bank. Frozen/untouched turns earn nothing, and progress carries across waves. Perfect Chest gives 1 item with one rare+ relic; Greed Chest gives 3 items with one epic+ relic; Ultra Chest gives 5 items with one legendary+ relic. Perfect uses yellow, Greed purple, and Ultra red consistently for progress and chest text. Only the highest earned tier can be claimed, and claiming resets the whole bank. Claims wait until the current action finishes. Final victory automatically opens the highest earned tier after its success-screen notice, retaining manual collection.

Relic-route clears spawn a closed chest on the battlefield. Tapping first gives it a brief 0.28-second shake, then enlarges it into the reel position and opens it automatically. Tap to stop: each tier has a **25%** upgrade chance; successes continue rolling through Perfect → Greed → Ultra. Each tier independently guarantees its fourth attempt after three failures, with hidden counters preserved across runs and restarts. Prizes and the complete upgrade chain are locked before animation; cosmetic taps do not change outcomes. Blood Gem and Soul routes pay directly after the same pre-opening shake. Soul chests scatter 20–48 glowing blue-white orbs, hover briefly, then fly to the Souls counter with staggered receipts. Level-spending is reflected in the animated counter; evolution visuals wait for collection. Chest Blood Gem receipts run at 1.6× speed with tighter staggering (about 0.9–1.3 seconds at normal speed), without changing enemy-drop timing. Wave-clear paid tarot draws are removed.

| Perfect | 1 | Rare+ | 50 |

A guarantee occupies **one randomly selected slot**. Its rarity rolls normally, with anything below its floor promoted to that floor. All other slots also contain relics; tarot and summon shards are excluded. Crystal payouts are separate and apply once on opening, including animation skips, with Soul Vessel bonuses. Relic rarity stays fixed at **58% common / 30% rare / 10% epic / 1% legendary / 1% cursed** at every wave; shop ordinary slots use the same fixed rates. Eligible relics are uniform within rarity; owned unique, incompatible Cursed Dice and fusion-only results are excluded. Exhausted rarity pools redistribute among eligible pools; a fully exhausted eligible relic pool converts that slot to 100 Blood Gems.

Opening a chest produces an impact pulse, rays and a large burst of crystals that bounce, settle, then fly to the currency counter. Reels show actual obtainable rewards in **one horizontal row in both layouts**, with tall neutral windows exposing neighboring symbols around a rarity-colored square. Reels appear in place at full size; Greed keeps the original centered while new reels enter from the sides. Each stopped prize emits a large rarity-colored explosion with bright sparks, thick trails, expanding rings and a brief local flash; every landing adds impact shake. Explosions scale from small Common bursts to full-size Legendary/Cursed bursts. Separate cosmetic RNG controls the show. Normal, Perfect and Greed reels slow down, slightly overshoot, and spring onto the prize; a second tap skips this finish. Ultra keeps crisp note-timed stops with no slowdown or overshoot. **Ultra reveals left to right on the final five notes** of `reward_level3.wav`: 3.000, 3.1875, 3.375, 3.5625 and 3.750 seconds. Its level-3 audio starts immediately on upgrading to Ultra (or opening an already-earned Ultra), with no extra stop tap; the finale remains unskippable. The full-tail `arcade_slot_roll.wav` (or `arcade_slot_roll2.wav` while the chest is Greed) repeats every nine sixteenth notes at 160 BPM (0.84375 seconds), with overlapping tails and sample-accurate loop timing. Every payout plays once: level 1 for Normal/Perfect and level 2 for Greed start with the first reward explosion; level 3 keeps the Ultra musical finale. Audio stops when leaving the chest. Collect gifts individually or **SKIP REMAINING** after taking any subset. Skipping preserves claimed gifts and crystals. Full inventories support replacing or leaving individual relics. New runs clear currencies, the bank and pending transactions.

Roll-changing relics and tarot effects are temporarily disabled and excluded from random rewards while the six-dice system is in use. Their design rows are marked light red in the primary document. The old category-weighted roll helpers remain for legacy simulations; live play uses independent dice.


Damage popups show the calculated hit before the remaining-health clamp, including lethal hits, burning, and Prismatic strikes. Actual HP loss and lifesteal remain capped by remaining HP. Squad instant kills show impact effects without damage numbers; city 111 and 666 remain numeric attacks. Number size is `24 × max(1, log10(damage))`, rounded to a whole pixel size, so 10 / 100 / 1,000 damage use 24 / 48 / 72. Other feedback keeps its regular size.

A shop route is first offered after wave 3, then only after two battle waves since the last shop visit. Shops occupy a wave without combat. Each offers **6 relics, 2 face-up tarots, and 1 sealed Soul Bottle**, with one random **Rare** relic guarantee. Fixed base prices are Common 100–150, Rare 180–230, Epic 280–320, Legendary/Cursed 400–500, and tarot 100–150 Blood Gems. Soul Bottles cost 100–300 Blood Gems and contain a hidden integer amount between ceil(price/20) and floor(price/10), locked when stocked and revealed on purchase. They grant souls immediately without taking an inventory slot; level-up visuals wait until returning to the battlefield. **50 Blood Gems refresh all nine listings**. Owned unique relics are excluded; full inventory requires replacement before payment. Leaving is free and opens the next route choice. The keeper arrives on the battlefield before transitioning into the storefront.

Shop merchandise sits on open shelves beside a hooded pixel shopkeeper. Tap to inspect, then use the large lower Buy button, or drag the listing onto it to purchase directly. Dropping elsewhere cancels. **Items / 背包** opens a ten-slot inventory in battle, shops and chest rewards: five enlarged rarity-colored items per row, including empty slots. Tap an owned item for its full description; a full-inventory purchase can be confirmed from that item's detail page.

## Tarot

The 22 tarot cards are single-use consumables in a separate, unlimited inventory with duplicates. Random choices and automatic grants use weight 1 per card, except The World at 0.5; bans renormalize the remaining pool. Automatic grants (generating relics, Empress and debug grants) briefly reveal the cards, then shrink them into the inventory icon in batches of up to three. Combat keeps running; settings, choices and other reward screens defer the animation. Hand-picked rewards keep their existing collection flight. Their effects can change rolls, react to roll outcomes, modify the next skill, or apply immediately. Tap the card icon to switch the dice area to the swipeable carousel; tap a card to inspect or drag it onto the battlefield to use it. The battlefield keeps running. Reward draws shuffle a deck from right to left, then fan it out; pick three, reveal them, and keep one. Tap anywhere during preparation to open the fan immediately, or during the three-card reveal to finish it. The skip tap is consumed. Draw the next card while earlier cards are still flying; there is no wait between picks. Drawn cards bob gently with staggered phases and remain still with reduced effects.

## Relics

Relics are obtained from chest slots and tarot effects using the fixed rarity rates above. A Game Coin held before clearing a wave grants its additional 10 blood gems once, before the new chest. All offers are resolved in the model before their visual reveal.

The inventory holds up to ten relics outside the shortened battlefield, below the raised player health bar. Sourced fantasy sprites sit on one translucent rounded bar, without visible cells or slot numbers. Rarity glows appear beneath enlarged/held inventory items, reward choices, pickups and chest reels: white common, blue rare, bright purple epic, peach legendary and red cursed. Resting inventory items have no rarity glow; the slightly larger icons sit on a lower bar. Treasure reels use stronger rarity glows and colored frames; revealed relics also show their rarity label. During spinning, color follows the visible symbol without exposing the locked reward. Frames use the same rarity palette; magic stones retain their elemental sprite colors. Hold a relic to show its description, including counts and stored charges; releasing dismisses it. The held icon enlarges slightly, moves only horizontally, and a grey rounded square marks its destination while neighbors preview the order. Small bottom-right counters show page stacks, stored charges, Reaper kills, and Cursed Bow extra hits. Charged Magic Shield and Magic Sword repeat a gold claim-style glow until their last stored charge is spent; reduced effects keeps a steady glow. Effects resolve **left to right**. With ten occupied slots, selecting a new relic asks which existing relic to replace. Chest pickups use the same replacement flow, with an option to leave that gift behind (no skip blood gems). Picking a relic plays a short lift-and-slam into its inventory position, with a bounce, impact ring and landing sound; stacks, replacements and fusions share the animation. Rewards commit once on selection, and progression waits for the landing. Codex Pages share one slot; the tenth fuses into Ancient Codex. All six magic stones fuse into Prismatic Stone at the earliest component position. Both fusion relics are excluded from drops, and their components stop appearing once the fused relic is owned. Inventory and relic counters reset on a new run.

All **53 relics** in the current design are implemented. Runestone is epic; Twin Scythes is legendary. Rarity rates move 10% of the remaining distance toward 20% each wave. Flat damage, health, armor, lifesteal, immunity, ordered damage modifiers, combo-hit modifiers and roll modifiers feed the shared combat model. Magic Shield and Magic Sword count six completed turns across waves, then store a whole-hit block or a ×3 critical for the next attack skill and all its hits. Saved bonus casts do not advance these counters. Last Contract prevents one death and restores 60% max HP. Reaper's Scythe counts enemy kills but excludes city troops. Prismatic Stone combines all six stones and adds a 100-base-damage strike on one random foe for each Perfect. Fortune Cookie grants a random tarot on acquisition and every three completed turns; Crystal Ball grants one each completed turn. Both respect tarot bans and carry counters across waves. Ancient Prophecy activates each used tarot twice, with fresh choices/targets when needed and the existing stacking/overwrite rules. The repeat cannot recursively repeat, even if Fool replaces the relic. A second impossible effect is skipped; the player may decline a repeat requiring input. Emperor/World resolve and lock each eligible reward before its picker. Full inventories open the shared backpack; inspecting a relic does not commit, and only the explicit replacement button consumes the card. Cancel keeps the original card and locked reward, returning that card to the front of the tarot fan. Cancelling a Prophecy repeat leaves its already accepted first gift intact. Full-inventory chest claims also open the backpack automatically; cancelling returns to the same unclaimed prize.

The new relics add flat multiplier bonuses (Fries, Hamburger), extra combo hits (Worn/Royal Dagger), additive pair/triple odds (Four/Five-leaf Clover), roll-triggered attacks (Worn Longbow, Tissue Pack, Magic Slingshot, Ghost and Dragon), attack healing (Bat), status modifiers (Lizard, Ice Rose, Slime), and retaliation/Perfect attacks (Spiked Helmet). Item attacks use the common shield/status/damage-event paths without spending the pending skill, Strength, Priestess or attack-boon charges. Fixed damage ignores damage bonuses; base damage uses the current DMG formula. Ice Rose has a recursion guard.

Disguise Mask copies its immediate left neighbor except Ancient Tentacle. Tentacle repeats every relic to its left, including Mask: `item → Mask → Tentacle` activates the item four times. Passive bonuses and trigger payouts repeat; turn counters advance once. Copied activations, multiplier flights and queued roll attacks animate at 1.5× speed, multiplying the existing battle playback speed. Reordering never grants health. Cursed Dice cover all six faces, but only one may be held; owned/reserved family members are excluded from subsequent drops. Soul Vessel adds 15% to all Blood Gem income per activation; fractional bonuses carry forward within the run. Ancient Prophecy copies each add one extra tarot activation, with the entire replay queue snapshotted to prevent recursion.


| Status | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Burning | 5 damage per stack after the enemy phase; capped at 10; loses 1 stack per turn |
| Freeze | Player breaks ice with 3 taps, ending the turn; enemies skip one action per freeze stack |
| Stun | Scrambles the next player throw; enemies cannot be stunned |
| Darkness | Halves outgoing damage and blocks healing; 1 stack by default unless an effect explicitly specifies more, loses 1 per turn |
| Corrosion | Disables defenses and increases incoming damage by 50%; 1 stack by default unless an effect explicitly specifies more, loses 1 per turn |
| Plague | Reduces max HP by 10 per stack to a minimum of 1; grows by 1 and spreads 1 stack to a random living, uninfected ally per turn; persists |

Tower applies one base stack per random application; Moon applies one permanent Darkness stack. Purple/Green Magic Stones, including Prismatic Stone, also apply one base stack. Explicit multi-stack skills remain supported. Runestone adds one application stack to subsequent status relics in inventory order. Enemy status icons appear below their health bars, enemy buffs (shield and next-attack boost) appear alongside ailments in hold inspection. Breaking a shield immobilizes a non-elite owner for one enemy action; elite actions and charging continue; corrosion or execution bypasses do not count as breaks. Freeze and shield-break locks overlap on the same skipped action. Plague spreads only to living allies without plague; newly infected allies neither receive a second spread nor spread it themselves that turn. Existing infections still gain one stack each turn. Player plague is disabled. Player statuses carry into the city, whose own health target remains immune.

## Twenty waves and weighted enemies

`scripts/progression/wave_catalog.gd` defines the nineteen squads from the design table, followed by the **20,000-HP city** on wave 20. General slots randomly select Vanguard, Archer or Mage; fixed slots create Paladins and High Priests at the specified elite tier. Enemy HP and damage multiply by `1.1^floor((wave-1)/2)`, rounded once from base stats: waves 1–2 ×1, 3–4 ×1.1, 5–6 ×1.21, through wave 19 ×2.357947691. Shop destinations still count as waves. Wave 6 has five normal enemies; wave 9 has two normal and three elite enemies; wave 16 has a High Priest and three elites; wave 18 has a Paladin, High Priest and three elites. Character health grows only through soul upgrades. Names remain hidden in battle; intent icons and hold descriptions show the locked next action.

Only valid skills enter each weighted draw: full health/shields and already-blessed targets cannot waste support choices. Freeze is excluded while the player is already frozen. Elemental status and support targets lock at round start. Healing cannot resurrect; blessing persists through support turns and applies across the next attack's full burst. All elites retain their initial 10 shield.

Paladins charge twice, then release two strikes with stun on the next turn. Elite High Priests chant twice, then release two strikes on the following turn. Charging becomes a committed sequence; freeze or immobilization cancels its progress and restarts from the first charge after recovery. Shield breaks only immobilize non-elites; elites keep their action and charge progress. Charge pips, a clock intent icon, hold descriptions and sequential impacts expose the sequence. Paladins and High Priests use distinct CC0 sprites, with gold and violet elite auras.

Living Paladins protect allies: targeted/random attacks and targeted tarot must select a Paladin. Area attacks still hit everyone, dealing full damage to Paladins and half to other enemies; area status effects still apply. **666 kills the entire squad as usual.** Target eligibility refreshes between hits when a Paladin dies.

Boons and afflictions share `StatusEffect`. Enemy attributes apply only when an attack removes player HP; shield, invincibility and invisibility block new afflictions as usual. Fireball adds one burning stack; High Priest elemental strikes apply two stacks of freeze, burn, stun, darkness or corrosion (never plague). Burning remains capped at ten, ticks for five damage per stack after the enemy phase, then loses one stack. Players break each frozen turn with three taps; stun affects the next accepted throw. The city remains immune to statuses.

## Debug dice

Open the **three-bar menu → Debug** to choose dice or load a wave by typing **1–20** and tapping **LOAD** (or pressing Enter). Loading starts a fresh run at that wave, with its matching full player health, size and enemy formation; wave 20 is the 20,000-HP city. It clears prior skills, statuses, treasure, combo, timers and animations. Invalid numbers leave the current run unchanged. The persistent Perfect toggle keeps its setting.

Open **Menu → Debug**, choose an illustrated double/triple or **Random Singles**, then charge and throw normally. Singles always have three different faces; doubles randomize the spare die. **Perfect: On** is a persistent toggle guaranteeing a Perfect on every accepted throw until switched off, snaps the marker to the center, and builds the Perfect combo without directly adding treasure. It can be combined with any selected result. The chosen dice combination applies once unless **Repeat** is on. Perfect remains on independently of Repeat, including after restart; use its toggle to turn it off. Canceled charging consumes neither setting. **Natural Random** restores independent six-sided dice. Explicit combinations override the odds even on a Perfect stop.

## Route rewards and soul evolution

Wave 1 randomly rewards Blood Gems, Souls, or a relic chest with equal probability. After each destination, choose one of two different battlefield arrows: Blood Gems, relics, Souls, or a battle-free shop. Collected relics remain visible and can be inspected or reordered while choosing. Eligible types are sampled equally; the same reward cannot occur three times in succession. A shop is offered after wave 3, then only after two battle waves since the last shop visit. Shops consume a wave and keep fixed prices and one rare guarantee.

Resource chests open on the battlefield: 100–400 Blood Gems or 10–40 Souls, with cumulative 10% and 1% upper bands at 200/20 and 300/30. Relic chests enlarge into the existing upgrade reels and open automatically. All chest item slots are relic-only; tarot and summon shards are removed.

Souls automatically buy levels 2–3 at incremental costs 30 and 50. Each multiplies character base damage by 1.5 (5/7.5/11.25) before flat relic bonuses and multiplies base maximum HP by 1.5 (100/150/225), healing the new capacity. Wave travel heals 25 but adds no maximum HP. The three original Flamelings forms reveal through queued silhouette transformations, starting with the former second form; the ember starter is retired. A new run resets levels and currencies.

Attack projectiles and impacts scale with the same `max(1, log10(damage))` factor as damage numbers, separately for each target; instant kills retain their tier effect. After all dice reveal, matching attacks wait 0.5 real seconds (menus pause this), then use 0.36 seconds travel and 0.11 seconds recovery before existing speed modifiers. When no straight remains and rerolls are available, the reroll label uses the existing bounce; without rerolls, End Turn bounces instead. Dice waves appear only on unused dice that can complete a valid straight with the current selection; longest-only idle hints are unchanged.

Selecting the upward route pans toward the upper right: paving and parallax props slide down-left, and enemies or the shopkeeper arrive from the upper right. The rightward route keeps horizontal travel. Both finish at normal battlefield positions; restart resets the scroll direction and accumulated vertical displacement.
